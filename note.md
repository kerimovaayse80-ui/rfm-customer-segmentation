# RFM Customer Segmentation — Methodology and Findings

## 1. Project Overview

This project applies RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to customer transaction data to identify customer segments and develop targeted marketing strategies.

RFM analysis evaluates customers using three dimensions:

- **Recency** — how recently a customer made a purchase
- **Frequency** — how often a customer placed an order
- **Monetary** — how much revenue a customer generated

The analysis was performed using the Kaggle Sample Sales Data dataset.

---

## 2. Dataset

**Dataset:** `sales_data_sample (1).csv`

The analysis identified **92 unique customers**.

The reference date was calculated as one day after the latest order date, resulting in **2005-06-01**.

---

## 3. RFM Calculation

### Recency

Recency measures the number of days since the customer's most recent order.

- Minimum: **1 day**
- Maximum: **509 days**
- Average: **182.83 days**

A lower Recency value represents more recent customer activity.

### Frequency

Frequency represents the number of unique orders placed by each customer.

- Minimum: **1 order**
- Maximum: **26 orders**
- Average: **3.34 orders**

### Monetary

Monetary represents the total sales generated by each customer.

- Minimum: **$9,129.35**
- Maximum: **$912,294.11**
- Average: **$109,050.31**

---

## 4. RFM Scoring

Each RFM metric was divided into four quantile groups using `pd.qcut()`.

### Recency Score

- **R = 4** → most recent customers
- **R = 1** → least recent customers

### Frequency Score

- **F = 4** → highest frequency
- **F = 1** → lowest frequency

### Monetary Score

- **M = 4** → highest monetary value
- **M = 1** → lowest monetary value

Customer ranking using `rank(method="first")` was applied before `qcut()` to handle tied values.

The three scores were combined into the `rfm_segment` code.

## 5. Customer Segments

### Champions

**Rule:** `R = 4, F = 4, M = 4`

**Customers:** 9

Champions are the highest-value and most recently active customers.

**Marketing strategy:** VIP rewards, exclusive offers, early access, premium loyalty benefits, and personalized high-value offers.

### Loyal

**Rule:** `R >= 3, F >= 3, M >= 3`, excluding Champions.

**Customers:** 17

Loyal customers show strong and consistent purchasing behavior.

**Marketing strategy:** loyalty programs, personalized offers, cross-selling, and repeat-purchase incentives.

### Needs Attention

Customers that do not meet the criteria for the other defined segments.

**Customers:** 54

This is the largest segment.

**Marketing strategy:** personalized discounts, product recommendations, reminder campaigns, engagement offers, and repeat-purchase incentives.

### At Risk

**Rule:** `R = 1, F >= 3, M >= 3`

**Customers:** 1

These customers were previously valuable or frequent but have become inactive.

**Marketing strategy:** urgent win-back campaigns, personalized discounts, limited-time incentives, and personalized email campaigns.

### Lost

**Rule:** `R = 1, F = 1, M = 1`

**Customers:** 11

Lost customers have low RFM scores and have not purchased recently.

**Marketing strategy:** reactivation campaigns, comeback offers, targeted promotions, and personalized email campaigns.

## 7. Visualization Findings

### Customer Segment Distribution

**Needs Attention** is the largest segment with 54 customers, representing the main opportunity for customer engagement.

### Recency vs Monetary

Champions are generally among the most recently active and highest-value customers. Customers with high Recency values are less recently active and may require retention or reactivation campaigns.

### Frequency vs Monetary

Customers with more orders generally tend to generate higher total sales. Champions include several high-frequency and high-monetary customers, while Lost customers generally have lower purchasing frequency and monetary value.

### RFM Score Heatmap

Champions have the strongest overall RFM scores, while Lost customers have low scores across all three dimensions. At Risk customers have low Recency scores but relatively stronger Frequency and Monetary scores.

### 3D RFM Segmentation

The 3D visualization provides an overall view of customers across Recency, Frequency, and Monetary dimensions and supports the rule-based segmentation.

## 8. Key Findings

The analysis identified five customer segments across 92 customers:

- **Champions:** 9 customers
- **Loyal:** 17 customers
- **Needs Attention:** 54 customers
- **At Risk:** 1 customer
- **Lost:** 11 customers

The main business priorities are:

1. Retain Champions.
2. Strengthen relationships with Loyal customers.
3. Increase engagement among Needs Attention customers.
4. Reactivate At Risk customers.
5. Test targeted win-back campaigns for Lost customers.
